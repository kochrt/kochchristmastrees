Our Trees

We have a nice selection of pine, spruce and fir trees:

  • Pine: Scotch Pine and White Pine
  • Spruce: Blue Spruce and Norway Spruce
  • Fir: Canaan Fir, Turkish Fir, Nordmann Fir, and a few White Fir and Douglas Fir

Most of our trees are in the 6–7 and 7–8 foot height range. We also have a few Canaan Fir in the 8–10 foot range.

If you would like a beautiful but less expensive tree, make an appointment for one of the last two weekends, when we’ll have our Norway and White Spruce trees tagged. Their needle retention is not as favorable as the pines and firs, but when cut later in the season, they can last through the New Year.

Only tagged trees are for sale. Untagged trees that are cut down are subject to a penalty.
