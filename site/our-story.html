Our Story

We are a family-owned tree farm located in picturesque Reily Township, in western Butler County, southwest Ohio. Our family has been selling quality cut-your-own Christmas trees since 2001.

We are a small nursery and are only open for a limited period of time. Please see our dates and hours before planning your visit.

We grow a selection of fir, pine and spruce trees. See the current season’s tree selection for this year’s varieties and sizes.

The tree brush we collect during harvesting season is used to make fresh wreaths for sale at our farm.

Hand saws are available to use, and our farm workers can assist you with cutting your tree. We shake the tree to remove debris and fit or drill the cut end for a tree stand. Tree wrapping is free of charge, and we have twine to attach your tree to your vehicle.

We also offer a variety of tree stands for sale and can help you select the best stand for your tree.

Have a Merry Christmas!
